Iran is a large country in the Middle East. Beside it lies a sea passage called the Strait of Hormuz. At its narrowest, it is about as wide as a long bike ride — around 33 kilometres. Huge ships full of oil squeeze through it every day.

Oil becomes petrol for cars, fuel for planes, and heat for houses. When the ships stop, oil gets harder to buy. Prices go up in shops and at petrol pumps far away from Iran.

The American president, Donald Trump, spoke on television. He said he is not in a hurry. He told Iran's leaders to give up. He said he has a secret line of contact to a powerful part of Iran's army. Nobody outside has checked that this is true. He also used rough words to threaten Oman, a neighbouring country, if it gets in America's way.

Iran's government answered calmly. One spokesman said the deadline was not such a big deal. Iran's foreign minister said no decision had been made about talking again. So both sides are waiting, and the ships are still not sailing.

伊朗是中东的一个大国。它旁边有一条海上通道，叫霍尔木兹海峡。最窄的地方只有大约三十三公里宽，差不多是骑一趟长途自行车的距离。每天都有装满石油的巨型油轮从这里挤过去。

石油会变成汽车的汽油、飞机的燃料、家里的暖气。油轮一停，石油就更难买到。就算离伊朗很远的地方，商店和加油站的价格也会往上涨。

美国总统特朗普在电视上说话。他说他不着急。他叫伊朗的领导人认输。他还说自己和伊朗军队中一个很有权力的部分有一条秘密联络渠道。外界无法证实这件事是真的。他又用很粗的话威胁邻国阿曼，说如果阿曼妨碍美国就要动手。

Why this matters为什么重要

The deadline was not a law or a treaty. It came from a memorandum of understanding — a signed statement of intent, closer to a handshake written down than to a binding contract — that President Trump put his name to in June at Versailles, outside Paris. It gave the two governments sixty days to turn a pause in fighting into a permanent settlement. On Monday, the sixty days ended. There is no settlement.

The most visible consequence sits in a stretch of water about the width of a long car journey at its narrowest point. The Strait of Hormuz is the only sea exit from the Persian Gulf, and in normal times roughly a fifth of the world's oil moves through it on tankers. It is still closed. When a route like that shuts, the effects do not stay at sea: shipping insurance becomes expensive or unavailable, cargoes are re-routed around longer paths, and the extra cost eventually reaches ordinary households through fuel, transport and the price of anything that travels.

Trump's message this week was that time is on his side. Speaking to Fox News, he said he has a direct backchannel — a private line of communication that bypasses official diplomacy — to Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, the branch of Iran's armed forces that answers to the country's supreme leader rather than to its elected government. He said he is 'not in a hurry,' told Tehran to wave the 'white flag,' and threatened, in language too crude to reproduce here, to bomb Oman if it interferes with American efforts in the Strait. Oman is a small Gulf state that has for decades hosted quiet talks between Washington and Tehran.

Two cautions belong with that paragraph. The existence of the backchannel is Trump's own claim; no one else has confirmed it. And CNN reported that it asked the White House to comment on the threat against Oman and had received no answer. A public threat against a country that is not a party to the conflict is unusual enough that its meaning is genuinely unclear — pressure tactic, negotiating theatre, or statement of intent.

Iran's public reaction was deliberately flat. Foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei played down the significance of the deadline, and Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said no decision had been made about resuming talks. That is a familiar posture: refusing to treat someone else's clock as your own is itself a form of leverage. Separately, NBC reported a European government document alleging that Russia is shipping drone components, ammunition and TNT to Iran across the Caspian Sea. That report rests on a single source and has not been independently confirmed; it should be held as a claim, not a fact.

What informed people argue about now is not what happened but what it means. One reading is that a deadline allowed to lapse without consequence teaches the other side that American deadlines are soft. The other is that walking away from an artificial date, rather than launching something you cannot control in order to honour it, is the more disciplined choice. Both readings are available from exactly the same set of facts, which is why the next few weeks will be read very differently depending on where you start.

Universities that refuse could lose government money for their laboratories.2026年8月18日（星期二），美国国防部给三十所大学寄了信。每封信都说同一件事：仔细检查你们和外国机构的所有合作，8月31日前交报告。不配合的大学，实验室可能会失去政府的经费。

Why this matters为什么重要America and China run the two biggest science systems on Earth, and for years their scientists have worked side by side. Universities are places where people study and where scientists do experiments. Much of that work is paid for by the government, using money collected from taxes.

Scientists like to work together across countries. A lab in America might share results with a lab in China. Two people far apart can solve a problem faster than one person alone.

The defence department worries that some of this sharing helps other governments, especially in ways that could matter for weapons. It keeps a list of foreign groups it does not trust. Now it wants each university to check its own partners against that list — and it gave them under two weeks.

Not everyone agrees this is the right move. A woman named Sarah Spreitzer, who speaks for American universities, said the deadline is very short and the rules are not clear. A member of Congress, John Moolenaar, said the checks are needed to keep America safe. The Pentagon did not say which 30 universities got letters.

大学是人们读书、科学家做实验的地方。这些研究很多是政府出钱，钱来自大家缴的税。

科学家喜欢跨国合作。美国的实验室可能会和中国的实验室分享结果。两个人隔得再远，一起想办法也比一个人快。

国防部担心，有些分享会帮到别国政府，特别是可能和武器有关的部分。它有一份不信任的外国机构名单。现在它要求每所大学拿自己的合作对象去对照那份名单——而且只给了不到两周。

并不是所有人都赞成。代表美国大学发言的斯普赖策女士说，期限太短，规则也不清楚。国会议员穆勒纳尔则说，这些检查是保护美国安全所必需的。五角大楼没有公布是哪三十所大学收到了信。

Disney Takes the Government to Court Over a TV Licence迪士尼为一张电视执照把政府告上法庭

On Tuesday 18 August 2026, Disney and its television network ABC sued a US government agency in a court in Washington DC. Disney says the agency is punishing ABC for the programmes it broadcasts. The agency has not answered in court yet.2026年8月18日（星期二），迪士尼和旗下的ABC电视网在华盛顿的联邦法院起诉了一个美国政府机构。迪士尼说，这个机构因为ABC播出的节目而惩罚它。该机构还没有在法庭上作出回应。

Why this matters为什么重要Almost everything you watch or read comes from a company that some government could squeeze. If a broadcaster can lose its licence for jokes about politicians, other broadcasters will think twice before telling you awkward news. This case asks who gets to decide what you are allowed to hear.你看的、读的几乎一切，都来自某个可能被政府施压的公司。如果一家电视台会因为拿政治人物开玩笑而丢掉执照，其他电视台在报道让人难堪的新闻前就会犹豫。这个案子要问的是：谁有权决定你能听到什么。
Read the whole story读完整篇

To broadcast television over the airwaves in America, a company needs a licence. A licence is official permission. It is a bit like a library card: the library lends you books, and it can take the card back if you break the rules.

The agency that hands out these licences is called the FCC. Licences must be renewed every few years. In April, the FCC told Disney to renew the licences for its eight television stations earlier than usual.

Disney says that is very strange. In its court papers, it says the FCC has not demanded an early renewal from anyone in more than 50 years. Disney also says government pressure grew until people were openly calling for ABC's licences to be taken away because of what its shows said.

Disney points to two ABC programmes: a daytime talk show called The View and a late-night comedy show hosted by Jimmy Kimmel. Both often make fun of politicians. Disney says punishing a broadcaster for its opinions breaks the First Amendment, a very old American rule protecting free speech. Everything about the government's reasons is Disney's claim so far. A judge will decide.

在美国，用无线电波播电视，公司必须有执照。执照就是官方许可，有点像图书证：图书馆借书给你，但你违反规定，它可以把证收回。

发放这类执照的机构叫FCC（联邦通信委员会）。执照每隔几年要更新一次。今年四月，FCC要求迪士尼旗下八家电视台提前提交更新申请。

迪士尼说这很反常。它在诉状里说，五十多年来FCC没有要求过任何人提前更新。迪士尼还说，政府的压力越来越大，最后有人公开要求因为ABC节目的内容而吊销它的执照。

迪士尼提到ABC的两档节目：白天的谈话节目《The View》，以及吉米·坎摩尔主持的深夜喜剧节目。两档节目常常拿政治人物开玩笑。迪士尼认为，因为观点而惩罚一家电视台，违反了美国保护言论自由的古老规则「第一修正案」。目前关于政府动机的说法，都只是迪士尼的指控。最后由法官来判断。

Words worth knowing值得记住的词
  • Broadcast licence广播执照 — Official permission from the government to send television or radio through the air.政府给的正式许可，允许用无线电波播出电视或广播。
  • Free speech言论自由 — The right to say what you think without the government punishing you for it.你可以说出自己的想法，政府不能因此惩罚你的权利。
